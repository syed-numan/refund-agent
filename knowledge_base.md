# ShopEasy Help Centre

## Refund Policy
Items can be returned for a full refund within 30 days of delivery. The item must be unused and in its original packaging, unless it arrived damaged or defective. Refunds go back to the original payment method.

## Refund Processing Time
Once a refund is issued it takes 5-7 business days to reach your bank account or card. UPI refunds usually arrive within 2-3 business days. Store credit is instant.

## High Value Refunds
Refunds above Rs 10,000 are reviewed by a supervisor before they are issued. This review usually takes less than 24 hours.

## Non-Refundable Items
Digital products (gift cards, software licences, e-books, subscriptions), personalised items, perishable goods and innerwear cannot be refunded.

## Damaged or Defective Items
If your item arrived damaged or stops working within 30 days, you are entitled to a full refund or a free replacement. Please keep photos of the damage, as our team may ask for them.

## Order Cancellation
Orders that have not been shipped can be cancelled for free from the My Orders page. Once an order has shipped it cannot be cancelled, but it can be returned after delivery.

## Return Pickup
After a return is approved, a courier picks up the item from your address within 2-3 days. Pickup is free of charge.

## Shipping
Standard delivery takes 3-5 business days. Express delivery takes 1-2 business days and costs Rs 99.

## Contacting a Human Agent
You can ask the chatbot to connect you to a human agent at any time. A support ticket is created and a human agent replies within 24 hours.
